# Ultimate Chronological Anime Timeline — CORRECTED (2026-10-28)

## How this version works (read this first)

The original list mixed three different kinds of dates together, which is why it
didn't make sense: real in-universe years, real-era analogies, and release
years. This version separates them with honest tags:

- **[Dated]** — the show itself gives a year or a real historical period.
- **[Analog]** — the setting is *inspired by* a real era but has no in-universe
  year. The era shown is the analogy, not a fact.
- **[Undated]** — no year and no single real era; placed by era-vibe, not by
  a fake date. Nothing here carries a year the show never gave it.

Nothing was dropped from the original list. Shows were moved, de-duplicated,
and re-tagged — not removed.

## Corrected watch math

- Total (original estimate, kept as-is): ~3,045+ hours
- Non-stop: 3,045 ÷ 24 = **~127 days** ✓ (original was right)
- At 2 hours/day: 3,045 ÷ 2 = 1,522 days = **~4.2 years** (original said 5.5 —
  wrong; 5.5 years at 2h/day would be ~4,015 hours)
- At 6 hours/day: 3,045 ÷ 6 = 507 days = **~1.4 years** (~1.5 with rounding ✓)

## What was fixed (the big ones)

- Rose of Versailles: ~1300 → late 1700s (pre-revolutionary France).
- Inuyasha: ~1100 → Sengoku period, 1500s.
- Rurouni Kenshin: listed twice (~1600 and ~1870s) → once, in Meiji 1870s–80s.
- King Arthur: ~400 BCE → ~500s CE legend analog.
- Lodoss War: ~400 BCE → medieval-analog fantasy (no real date).
- Reign: The Conqueror: ~3000 BCE → ~330 BCE (Alexander lived 356–323 BCE).
- Girls und Panzer: 1940s–50s → modern day (school sport, present).
- Hellsing: 1930s–40s → modern day with a WW2 backstory.
- Attack on Titan: ~1940s → undated walled society (19th-century-level tech).
- Akira: 1980s → 2019 (Neo-Tokyo in-universe).
- Bubblegum Crisis: 1980s → 2032–33 (MegaTokyo in-universe).
- Cowboy Bebop: 1998 → 2071 (1998 was the release year, not the setting).
- Soul Eater: 2977–3200s → post-Fire Force remade world (corrected twice:
  first wrongly grouped with Captain Harlock, then wrongly called
  modern-day — see Future, year unknown).
- Gurren Lagann: "~10,000 years" → far-future, undated (and moved before
  Diebuster, since 10k < 12k in the original order was flipped).
- Now and Then, Here and There: "~10 billion years" → far-future dying
  world, undated (the show gives no year).
- Expelled from Paradise: "~2,400 years (post-12k)" → future digital world,
  undated.
- All racing entries folded into their eras (they were separated by vehicle
  type, which broke chronology).
- Mystery entries folded into their eras (same reason).
- Blood+: flagged multi-era (main story modern day, flashbacks 1940s–2000s).
- Added 2026-10-29: Bleach and Digimon Adventure (1999) to the
  Contemporary section (both [Undated]); neither was in the original
  list or the Google roster draft.

## Theme thread (kept from the original)

Isolation / trapped runs the whole length of this list: walled societies
(Attack on Titan), trapped islands (Umineko), trapped in a game
(Log Horizon), domed cities (Ergo Proxy), deep space (Bebop, Harlock),
and a spirit world cut off from time (Spirited Away). That thread is the
real spine of the list — stronger than any single year on it.
