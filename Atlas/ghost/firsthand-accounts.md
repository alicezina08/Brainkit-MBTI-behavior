**Shared 2026-11-18. Alice's own experiences, recorded in her words, offered by her as material for BrainKit's ghost data — shared publicly by her choice.**

## 1. The childhood home — severely haunted

"Childhood home was severe haunted. Old model items that made sound they wound up to play with play on there own. We had a nightmare of a teddy ruxphin. No batteries no tape in it. But would be able to say things on its own even after the cords were gutted. And in the closet. Had a wake up one morning with my blanket levitating a foot or so above me I never left my room faster then anything before. Fans at night you heard it making voices."

**What this grounds:** a toy that speaks with no power source and its cords gutted; objects that play on their own; a blanket levitating a foot above a sleeping child; fans forming voices at night. This is poltergeist-type activity — objects moving and sounding on their own — and disembodied voices. It grounds the Poltergeist profile's physical moveset (objects moving, things speaking) and the Intelligent Haunting profile's voices.

## 2. The next house — a hundred times worse

"The next house we moved into was 100 times worse. I even scared the hell out of my sister. When we weren't at home I explained to her what I was hearing at night she didn't believe me so I told her the next night I here it I'll come wake u up to come watch anime or play a game. Well it happened I heard voices everyone else asleep it wasn't the TV it was coming from my sister's room. She was a sleep in a dark room no radio no TV. In that room it should have been impossible to hear males and group of people's voices. I got up walked to her room opened door as I did I heard the spirits quite down. Went over to wake her up as planed she came out of the room we went to living room I turned the TV back to playing the show. And not even minutes later her room had loud voices coming from it like yelling arguing ect it didn't stop for another hour and no one in the house heard it but me and my sister who was now freaking out and never wanted in that room again."

**What this grounds:** disembodied voices in a dark room with no radio and no TV — men's voices and groups of people, yelling and arguing for an hour — heard by two people (Alice and her sister) and by no one else in the house. The voices quieted when Alice opened the door, then returned louder once they had left the room. This grounds the Intelligent Haunting profile's disembodied voices and the way a haunting can focus on one room and be heard only by the people in it.

## 3. The graveyard stairs — witnessed by her whole family

"My experience at a grave yard walking down flight of stairs my family seen me fly up into the air as if I was pushed then something flipped me right up in the air and set me back on the steps safely. Brother mom and dad witnessed it."

**What this grounds:** a physical lifting — Alice, walking down a flight of stairs in a graveyard, was seen by her brother, her mother, and her father to fly up into the air as if pushed, then flipped right up in the air and set back down on the steps safely. Four people witnessed the same event. This grounds the Poltergeist profile's physical moveset at its strongest: a force that can lift and move a person — and the detail that matters most, that it set her back down safely.
